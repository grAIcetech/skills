# /beautify

Turn a rough idea into a complete, copy-paste "master prompt" design brief for a
genuinely beautiful website, app, or interface — through a short, guided interview
instead of a blank page.

## What It Does

- Interviews you one short round at a time — by voice or in chat — through eight
  focused questions: outcome & success feeling, audience & emotional job, product
  truths & primary conversion, visual references, creative freedom, imagery standard,
  anti-goals, and process/approval gates.
- Restates what it captured after each round so you can correct it immediately.
- Fills a proven master-prompt template from your answers — never invents a product
  truth, price, or claim; unanswered fields are marked, not guessed.
- Delivers the finished brief as one copy-paste block, ready to paste into an AI
  design tool (Ploy, v0, Lovable, etc.) or hand to a coding agent (Codex, Claude Code).

## Why

Most AI design output looks generic because the brief that produced it either said too
little (`"make it beautiful"`) or too much (a fully pre-designed page in prose, which
forces the agent to defend that direction even when the emotional result is wrong). The
fix is a brief that gives **truth, taste, and boundaries** — what must be true, who
must feel something, what "excellent" looks like through concrete references, what
must never happen, and where the agent is free to surprise you. This skill is that
brief's structure, asked as questions so you never have to remember the shape yourself.

## When To Use It

Use it when you say things like:

- "/beautify" or "/beautiful"
- "Help me write a design brief for this site."
- "I want to brief Ploy/Codex/Claude on making this beautiful, not generic."
- "Walk me through the questions instead of me writing the prompt myself."

npx @agent-native/skills@latest add --skill beautify
